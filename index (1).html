About

Hey — I'm Manish Rana, an ethical hacking and cybersecurity student based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I study at Softwarica College of IT & E-Commerce, pursuing a BSc (Hons) in Ethical Hacking & Cybersecurity validated by Coventry University, UK.

My approach is methodical and curiosity-driven. I like to understand how systems work at a deep level — protocols, memory, process flows — so I can find exactly where they break. Then I document it, report it, and help fix it.

I spend time on penetration testing labs, building security automation tools in Python, and studying networking fundamentals with Cisco Packet Tracer. My GitHub shows the coursework I'm proud of — IDS systems, OSINT tools, network simulations.

When I'm not in the terminal, I'm researching new CVEs, reading writeups, or leveling up on platforms like TryHackMe and HackTheBox. The goal is simple: become a professional who makes digital systems safer.
